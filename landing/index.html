The idea

The idea

Nothing here lasts.

Every 40 days, the feed empties itself. Posts, replies, reposts, likes, photos, video: permanently deleted, no way back.

Say the honest thing. The clock forgives you.

The wipe

What dies. What lives.

Dies

  • Posts, replies, reposts
  • Likes
  • Media: photos and video
  • Hashtags
  • Notifications

Row by row. Blob by blob. No archives, no recovery.

Lives

  • People and follows
  • Game scores
  • The wipe history itself

The community survives. The content does not.

The contract

The wipe cannot be skipped.

  1. 01

    One source of truth: a single 40-day constant. The countdown, the API, and the scheduler all read the same number.

  2. 02

    Ask for the countdown and an overdue purge runs first. No cron, no problem: the clock enforces itself.

  3. 03

    No visitor can trigger it. The wipe fires on the grid, or by an admin key. Never by a tap.
