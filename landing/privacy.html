Privacy policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026. A baseline policy, to be replaced before launch.

The short version

This landing page collects nothing. No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no ads, no external fonts. The countdown and the ash run entirely in your browser.

The notify form

The email form on the home page is a front-end placeholder and is not yet connected to any backend. Nothing you type is transmitted or stored.

Hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare. Like most infrastructure providers, they may log basic technical request data (such as IP addresses) to operate their services. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Changes
