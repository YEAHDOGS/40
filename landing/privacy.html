Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026. A baseline policy, to be replaced before launch.

The short version

This landing page collects one thing, and only if you give it: the email address you put on the waitlist. No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no ads, no external fonts. The countdown and the ash run entirely in your browser.

The waitlist

When you join the waitlist, your email address is sent to the DOGS API (api.dogs.red, a Cloudflare Worker) and stored in a Cloudflare D1 database with the time you joined. It is used for one thing: telling you when 40Forty opens. It is not sold or shared. Submitting the form loads Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that a person sent it; it shows no puzzle and is not loaded unless you submit the form.

Hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare. Like most infrastructure providers, they may log basic technical request data (such as IP addresses) to operate their services. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Changes

When 40Forty launches for real, this policy will be replaced with a complete one before anything more is collected.
